Biography

I am currently a PhD Scholar under Yogesh K.Meena at the HAIx Lab, IIT Gandhinagar, and hold an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from NIT Hamirpur.

My academic journey is driven by a passion for unraveling the mysteries of genetics and leveraging computational power to solve complex challenges in healthcare. I actively seek opportunities to contribute to groundbreaking research and innovation at the intersection of biology and technology.

Research Focus

My work primarily focuses on AI for Healthcare, Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI), and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).

My philosophy revolves around bridging the gap between theoretical machine learning and real-world biological applications. Whether it is decoding EEG signals for electric wheelchair control or analyzing X-rays for disease detection, I am dedicated to developing scalable, efficient, and interpretable AI models.
